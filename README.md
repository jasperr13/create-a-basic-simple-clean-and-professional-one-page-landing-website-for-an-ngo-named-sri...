# Create a basic, simple, clean, and professional one-page landing website for an NGO named Sri...

Create a basic, simple, clean, and professional one-page landing website for an NGO named “Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas.”

The NGO is dedicated to helping underprivileged children, students, elderly people, and people in need by providing education, healthcare, care, shelter, and support.

Website Style

Create a design that feels:

Simple and modern

Peaceful and trustworthy

Warm and welcoming

Easy to understand

Mobile responsive

Not overly fancy or complicated

Use a white/light cream background with subtle saffron/orange and navy blue accents.

Use clean typography, rounded buttons, soft shadows, and good spacing.

1. Navbar

Create a simple sticky navigation bar.

Left side:
Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas

Right side navigation:

Home

About Us

Our Mission

Services

Contact

Add a highlighted “Support Us” button.

Clicking navigation links should smoothly scroll to the relevant section.

2. Hero Section

Create a large, clean hero section.

Main heading:

Serving Humanity with Love, Care & Compassion

Supporting text:

Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas is dedicated to supporting children, students, elderly people, and those in need through education, healthcare, care, and community support.

Add two buttons:

Learn More

Support Our Mission

Include a warm NGO/community image or simple visual showing education, care, community, or helping people.

3. About Us

Heading:

About Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas

Text:

Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas is a non-profit initiative created with the vision of serving humanity and supporting people who need care and opportunities.

Our aim is to create a safe and supportive environment where children can receive education, elderly people can receive proper care, and people in need can receive help and support with dignity and respect.

Keep this section simple with text on one side and an appropriate image on the other.

4. Our Mission

Heading:

Our Mission

Add a short description:

Our mission is to serve humanity by providing essential support, opportunities, care, and compassion to those who need it most.

Create 4 simple cards:

Free Education

Providing education and learning opportunities to children and students who need support.

Healthcare Support

Helping people receive proper medical attention, treatment, and healthcare support.

Elderly Care

Providing care, respect, companionship, and support to elderly people.

Community Support

Helping families and individuals in need through care, resources, and community assistance.

Use simple icons for each card.

5. Our Vision

Heading:

A Better Future Through Seva

Text:

We believe that education, healthcare, compassion, and selfless service can create meaningful change in society. Our vision is to build a community where nobody feels alone or helpless.

Add a simple inspiring image beside the text.

6. Support / Call to Action

Create a visually highlighted section.

Heading:

Together, We Can Make a Difference

Text:

Every act of kindness can change someone's life. Join us in our mission to support children, elderly people, and communities in need.

Add buttons:

Support Us

Become a Volunteer

For now, these buttons can scroll to the Contact section.

7. Contact Section

Heading:

Get in Touch

Add a simple contact form with:

Full Name

Email

Phone Number

Message

Send Message button

Also create placeholders for:

Email

Phone

Address

Do not invent real contact details. Display “Coming Soon” or make them easy to replace later.

8. Footer

Create a simple footer containing:

Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas

Serving Humanity with Love, Care & Compassion

Quick links:
Home | About | Mission | Services | Contact

Add:

© 2026 Sri Guru Ram Das Ji Niwas. All Rights Reserved.

Technical Requirements

Build it as a responsive single-page landing website.

Make it look good on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Keep the code clean and well structured.

Use smooth scrolling.

Add subtle hover effects and simple animations.

Avoid excessive animations.

Keep loading performance fast.

Use reusable components.

Use Lucide icons where suitable.

Do not add login/signup functionality.

Do not create an admin dashboard.

Do not add payment integration yet.

Do not add unnecessary features.

Keep this first version focused on being a basic professional NGO landing page.
